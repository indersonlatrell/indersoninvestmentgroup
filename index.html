About

Where discipline
meets opportunity.

Founded on a belief that markets reward preparation over intuition, Inderson Investment Group has built a systematic approach to trading forex and futures markets across global time zones.

We combine proprietary algorithmic signals with seasoned discretionary oversight — ensuring our capital deployment is both data-driven and contextually aware of macro conditions.

Forex Markets
Major, minor, and exotic currency pairs across London, New York, and Tokyo sessions. Specializing in momentum and mean-reversion strategies.
Futures Markets
Commodity, index, and interest rate futures. Positioned to benefit from both trending and ranging macro environments.
Risk Management
Every position is governed by strict drawdown limits, position sizing protocols, and portfolio-level correlation controls.

Strategy

Three pillars of
sustainable alpha.

01

Systematic Research

Quantitative models screen thousands of market conditions daily, identifying high-probability setups with asymmetric risk-reward profiles across correlated instruments.

02

Macro Alignment

All positions are filtered through a top-down macro framework — ensuring our trade thesis is consistent with broader central bank policy, liquidity cycles, and geopolitical flows.

03

Dynamic Risk Control

Volatility-adjusted position sizing, real-time drawdown monitoring, and portfolio heat mapping ensure capital preservation remains the foundation of every allocation decision.
